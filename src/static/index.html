What is Adalat Pulse?

                        
Automated Courtroom Telemetry & Koyal ASR Quality Assurance

                    

                

                
            


            

                

                    
1. The Mission

                    
Adalat AI deploys Indic Speech-to-Text (Koyal ASR) across 11 partner state High Courts and District Courts, transcribing hearings live for 6,000+ judges. If courtroom hardware or network fails mid-hearing, official court proceedings stall.

                


                

                    
2. Mandatory Pre-Flight Quality Gates

                    
Before any judge takes the bench, Adalat Pulse runs a 5-second deterministic hardware and network evaluation against certified golden baselines:

                    
    
                        
  • Network Latency: < 200 ms (Critical breach at ≥ 300 ms)
    • 
                        
  • Packet Loss: < 1.0% (Critical breach at > 2.0%)
    • 
                        
  • Jitter: < 30 ms (Critical breach at > 40 ms)
    • 
                        
  • Audio Hardware: ≥ 16,000 Hz sample rate required
    • 
                        
  • Room Acoustics: ≥ 18 dB SNR (Room noise < 55 dBA)
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
3. Automated P0 Incident Routing

                    
When any courtroom breaches critical quality gates, Adalat Pulse automatically halts the session, routes an urgent Block Kit alert to Slack (#all-adalat-pulse-alerts), and files a priority Linear issue (Team ALL) within 2 seconds.
